Thank you for reviewing KinDee: Thai Calorie Tracker. We respectfully believe the Guideline 4.3(a) finding is a misunderstanding, and we would like to explain what makes KinDee distinct.

1. Original work, not a template
KinDee was designed and written by our own small team in Thailand. It is not built from a purchased or repackaged app template, it does not share source code or assets with any other app, and we have not submitted similar apps under this or any other developer account. The app name, icon and artwork are our own.

2. Built specifically for Thai food and Thai users
Most calorie trackers rely on Western food databases, where Thai dishes are missing or listed in Western units. KinDee is built around Thai food:
- Its own catalogue of 1,876 Thai foods: 1,133 dishes, 591 drinks, 66 desserts, 42 fruits and 44 convenience-store items. Each has calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat, and the whole catalogue is built into the app and works fully offline.
- Portions use the units Thai people actually order and eat: plate (จาน), bowl (ชาม), cup (ถ้วย), glass (แก้ว), piece, bottle, box and pack, rather than only grams.
- A Thai-language search engine we wrote for this app. It ignores tone marks and spaces and matches commonly confused Thai spellings (for example ำ/าม, ใ/ไ, ทร/ซ, ณ/น), so users can find "ข้าวกะเพรา" however they type it.
- A Thai-only interface, written for Thai users rather than machine-translated.

3. A different approach to tracking
KinDee works as a daily calorie "budget" based on the user's own TDEE, worked out from age, sex, height, weight and activity level. The user sees what they have left to eat today, and a calendar shows how they kept to the budget over time. Barcode scanning (via the open Open Food Facts database) and manual entry fill the gaps. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics SDKs or in-app purchases. All data stays on the device, and the user can export it or delete it at any time.
